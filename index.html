Battering Intervention and Prevention Program (BIPP)

18 weeks36 hoursEnglishEspañolZoom or in person

An 18-week program that satisfies the 36-hour TDCJ-CJAD and State of Texas requirement. Four phases, offered in both Spanish and English, delivered through interactive Zoom classes or in person. Groups run at 5 to 20 people under State of Texas BIPP Accreditation Guidelines.

BIPP addresses socio-cultural issues regarding gender and violence, critical thinking, empathy, accountability, and communication. Completion requires the minimum 36 hours of educational group sessions, appropriate participation, and payment of fees.

Affirmative Finding of Family Violence (AFV) Counseling is available where the court does not recommend BIPP but does order family violence counseling.

BIPP is not an anger management program. If your court order says BIPP, anger management will not satisfy it. If you are unsure which program your order requires, call and we will help you read it.

A note on couples counseling. The clinic advises against couples counseling until the abusive party has completed an appropriate intervention program and the other partner feels safe to participate.

Start here

BIPP intake in four steps

One phone call starts the process. Bring your court paperwork and the name of your probation officer or referral source.

  1. Register

    Call 915-799-0614 to schedule your evaluation and orientation.

  2. Evaluation

    A psycho-social evaluation with a clinician.

  3. Orientation

    An interactive group session that introduces the program and its rules.

  4. Ongoing groups

    Weekly groups of 5 to 20 people, in English or Spanish, until the 36 hours are complete.

What the program requires of you

  • Firearms. All firearms must be removed from the home during participation.
  • Release of information. Each participant signs a release allowing BIPP to contact the victim and the referral source.
  • Rules. Participants read, sign, and adhere to Capelli Group Rules.
  • Fees. TDCJ-CJAD requires accredited BIPP programs to charge participants, reinforcing that abusive behavior has consequences. Fees are set at registration.

What happens on your behalf

  • Victims are notified of enrollment and of exit from the program.
  • Monthly progress reports are mailed to your referral source.
  • Completion requires the minimum 36-hour educational group sessions, appropriate participation, and payment of fees.
