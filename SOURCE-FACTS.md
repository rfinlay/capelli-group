## Services

### 1. BIPP — Battering Intervention and Prevention Program
- **18-week program**, satisfies the **36-HOUR TDCJ/CJAD & State of Texas requirement**
- Four phases available in **both Spanish and English**
- Delivered via **Zoom Platform Interactive Classes** (online) and in person
- **Group session schedule (verbatim):**
  - Monday 6:00 – 8:00 pm — Men's English Group
  - Tuesday 6:00 – 8:00 pm — Men's Spanish Group
  - Wednesday 6:00 – 8:00 pm — Women's English Group
  - Saturday 11:00 am – 1:00 pm — Men's English Group
- **Four-step intake:** 1) Register — call 915-799-0614 to schedule evaluation and orientation.
  2) Evaluation — psycho-social evaluation. 3) Orientation — interactive group session.
  4) Ongoing Groups — 5-20 people per State of Texas BIPP Accreditation Guidelines.
- Completion requires the minimum 36-hour educational group sessions, appropriate participation,
  and payment of fees.
- **Requirements (verbatim intent):** all firearms removed from the home during participation;
  each participant must sign a release of information allowing BIPP contact with the victim and
  referral source; victims notified of enrollment and exit; monthly progress reports mailed to
  the referral source; participants must read, sign and adhere to Capelli Group Rules.
- **Important distinction stated on the site:** "BIPP is not an anger management program."
  It addresses socio-cultural issues regarding gender and violence, critical thinking, empathy,
  accountability, and communication.
- The site advises against couples counseling until the abusive party has completed an
  appropriate intervention program and the other partner feels safe to participate.
- **Affirmative Finding of Family Violence (AFV) Counseling** available where BIPP is not
  recommended by the courts but family violence counseling is.
- TDCJ-CJAD requires accredited BIPP programs to charge participants for services as a way of
  reinforcing that abusive behavior has consequences. (No BIPP price is published — do not invent one.)

## CRITICAL SAFETY CORRECTION

The live site publishes **1-800-273-8255 (TALK)** as a crisis line.

**That number was retired.** The National Suicide Prevention Lifeline transitioned to the
**988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline** in July 2022. The old 10-digit number still forwards today, but
**988 is the current published national standard.**

**In the rebuild, use 988 as the primary crisis number.** Also include the **Crisis Text Line
(text HOME to 741741)** and the **Veterans Crisis Line (988, then press 1)** — the veterans line
is directly relevant given the Fort Bliss and Military Veteran Safe Zone positioning.

**Do NOT include a local El Paso crisis or shelter number.** I have not verified one, and an
incorrect crisis number on a domestic violence page is the single worst possible defect.
National numbers only — those are verifiable and stable.
